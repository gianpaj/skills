## How to Use This Reference

When processing a new invoice:

1. **Find the vendor name** from the invoice (use the full legal name)
2. **Look up in this table** for exact matches or similar vendors
3. **Use the recommended category** and VAT %
4. **If not in table**, apply these defaults:
   - EU companies with digital services → Cloud services, 0% VAT
   - Physical products from EU → 21% VAT
   - Services from EU → 19-21% VAT
   - International companies → Check invoice for VAT treatment

## Adding New Vendors

When you encounter a vendor not in this list, research:

1. Full legal company name and country
2. Service type (cloud, hardware, service)
3. VAT treatment (check invoice for "VAT", "IVA", "Impuesto")
4. Typical monthly/annual cost (for forecasting)

Then add to this reference for future invoices.
